# The Pangolin Code

*Thirteen commandments for shipping bravely and staying sealed.*

Security isn't the opposite of bold. A pangolin forages in the open, then curls
into armor nothing gets through. Be that. Share everything worth sharing — and be
completely unbothered about the parts you keep locked.

---

**I. Forage before you flee.**
Know every secret in your tree before it leaves the burrow.

**II. When in doubt, curl up.**
If a file can't be proven safe, it doesn't ship. Fail closed, always.

**III. Never half-scrub.**
A file goes whole or not at all. A redacted secret is a secret that survived.

**IV. Keep every receipt.**
Manifest, audit, and hash ride with the share — or it isn't a share.

**V. Same tree, same seal.**
If the payload didn't change, neither does the fingerprint.

**VI. The `.secret` stays secret.**
What lives in the vault never travels. Not once. Not "just this time."

**VII. Overrides sign their name.**
Bend a rule and it shows up in the fingerprint. No silent bypass.

**VIII. Verify, never trust.**
A seal you didn't check is a rumor.

**IX. Ship the evidence with the artifact.**
Your source should travel like a good model ships its weights — with proof.

**X. Be a little rebellious.**
Move fast. Break nothing that matters.

**XI. Secure isn't the same as scared.**
Guard everything. Flip off the fear.

**XII. Bare it all — except the secrets.**
Radical in the open, locked where it counts.

**XIII. Leave the burrow braver.**
The whole point is to share the thing. Go.
