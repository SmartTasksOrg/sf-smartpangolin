What it does

Two instincts, one pass.

A pangolin forages out the smallest hidden things, then rolls into a shell nothing gets through. The tool does both to your repo, in a single run.

01 · forage

Find what shouldn't ship

Anchored rules hunt out credential files, inline tokens, and local-infrastructure disclosure. On a hit it excludes the whole file — never a half-redaction a secret can survive — and records the rule, offset, and length. Never the value.

02 · seal

Prove what did

What remains is sealed into an artifact with a manifest, an exclusion audit, the exact policy that ran, and two hashes: one for the payload, one for the bytes. Pin the clock and the zip is reproducible to the byte.

Ship the evidence with the artifact

Every share is auditable by design.

The matched secret is never written anywhere — only its rule, offset, and length — so the audit record itself is safe to share.
